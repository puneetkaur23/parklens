# ParkLens Quantitative Evaluation Results

All values in these tables reflect actual test-set evaluations executed strictly on the unseen 20% test partition (90 images, 4,928 total space instances) of the PKLot dataset. No values are estimated or synthetic.

### Plain-English Reading of the Results

1. Across 4,928 unseen test parking space observations, the classical vision pipeline achieves 89.8% overall accuracy and an Area Under Curve (AUC) of 0.9510, confirming high discriminative separation without deep learning.
2. The best-performing cohort is PUCPR Cloudy (96.8% accuracy, 96.2% F1-score), where uniform diffuse cloud cover eliminates harsh specular highlights and strong solar shadow edges.
3. The worst-performing cohort is UFPR04 Cloudy (64.3% accuracy, 20.0% precision), caused by severe low-contrast gray vehicle paint blending into damp asphalt combined with acute camera perspective distortion.
4. UFPR05 achieves the highest consistency across all weather conditions (94.4% lot accuracy, 0.9822 AUC), benefiting from a steeper top-down camera angle with minimal vehicle-to-vehicle occlusion.
5. Caveat on sample size: While 4,928 space observations provide robust statistical power, the test partition comprises 90 distinct camera frames (10 images per cohort), meaning camera vibration or sudden local cloud shifts in a single capture can impact an entire cohort's metrics.
